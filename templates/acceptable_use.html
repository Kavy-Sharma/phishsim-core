1. Authorized Target Verification

Simulations may only be targeted towards domains owned, leased, or explicitly authorized by your organization. PhishSim AI verifies domain ownership through DNS records or corporate single sign-on.

2. Prohibited Exploitation

Customers may not weaponize templates with live malware, exploit payloads, malicious attachments, or real credential phishing pages targeting third-party services outside authorized test pretexts.

3. Safe Mailbox Testing

Campaigns dispatched through the sandbox environment route through isolated relays and never reach untracked public mailboxes.
