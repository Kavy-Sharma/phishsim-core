1. Data Ingestion & Sanitization

All target lists and recipient attributes are sanitized upon ingestion. Special characters and extraneous fields are discarded to ensure data hygiene and security.

2. Encryption Standards

All data is secured using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Key rotation is automated every 90 days across all production databases.

3. Access Controls

Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures only designated workspace administrators can view campaign telemetry and training results.
