Data Handling Policy

Clear, direct parameters explaining how simulation telemetry, employee identifiers, and risk metrics are managed.

1. Core Telemetry & Employee Identifiers

PhishSim.ai operates as an automated spear-phishing simulation and remediation platform. To run simulations, we require target employee email addresses and names.

  • Data Collection: We collect and store names, emails, and departments for target lists uploaded by account administrators.
  • Data Usage: This data is used exclusively to generate, personalize, and route simulated training emails.
  • Retention: Targets and campaign logs are retained inside your secure tenant database and can be purged immediately at the administrator's request.

2. Sandbox Dispatching & Email Security

PhishSim.ai supports three distinct sending tiers to accommodate varying compliance levels:

  • Sandbox Mode: Telemetry is routed through a local mock SMTP server (smtp4dev). No external emails leave the network, ensuring zero exposure for local training assessments.
  • Safe Send: Telemetry is routed to Mailtrap SMTP sandboxes. Emails are trapped inside sandboxed mailboxes, preventing them from ever reaching actual customer inboxes.
  • SMTP Live Mode: Live emails are delivered to company-allowlisted SMTP inboxes under strict, pre-configured authorized consent.

3. Payload & Credential Handling (No Harvesting)

PhishSim.ai values workforce trust and operational compliance above all else. Under no circumstances does the platform harvest real credentials.

  • No Credential Capture: Simulated landing pages capture click events and submit clicks. Real employee passwords, MFA tokens, or financial records are **never** logged, stored, or transmitted.
  • Metadata Telemetry: We only log click timestamps, client IP ranges, and user agents to formulate human risk index analytics.

4. Payment & Billing Integrity

All subscription upgrades and financial operations are processed via Stripe's secure infrastructure. Credit card numbers, card verification values (CVV), or bank statements are never transmitted through or cached on PhishSim.ai databases.
