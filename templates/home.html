Core Platform Capabilities

CSV Campaign Launch

Upload an employee CSV and provide your company domain. PhishSim parses departments and job titles to prepare a tailored simulation — no manual setup needed.

Agentic Email Generation

The AI drafts a unique, context-aware phishing email for each employee. Each message references their role, department, and company tone — generated fresh, every time.

Real-Time Dashboards

Live metrics track emails sent, open rates, click rates, and how many employees correctly reported the simulation — all updated as events happen.

AI Risk Analysis

After a campaign, the AI analyzes the click-rate data, identifies the most vulnerable departments, and writes a plain-language risk summary with actionable next steps.

Security Reports

Each completed campaign generates a detailed security posture report — highlighting what worked, who clicked, and what training to prioritize next.

PDF Export

Download a professionally formatted PDF report in one click — ready for compliance audits, board reviews, or sharing with your security team.
