The Attack Chain

Anatomy of an
Autonomous Attack

Scroll down to watch the Agentic AI orchestrate a full-scale social engineering campaign. Each step is fully autonomous, dynamically reacting to targets.

01

OSINT Gather

The agent scrapes your company domain, LinkedIn data, and public records to build a complete target profile — identifying departments, executives, and hierarchy.

02

AI Crafting

Using the OSINT data, the LLM generates a unique, personalized phishing email per employee. A junior dev gets a Jira ticket alert, while Finance gets an urgent wire request.

03

Delivery

Emails are dispatched via SMTP directly to real employee inboxes. Each email carries a unique tracking pixel and click-tracking token to monitor engagement.

04

Real-Time Tracking

Every open, click, and report event is logged in real-time to your dashboard. You see exactly who is vulnerable — as it happens, not days later.

05

AI Risk Report

The AI analyzes campaign data, identifies the most exposed departments, and generates a formatted PDF risk report ready for compliance and board reviews.

06

Risk Remediation

The final layer converts raw click and report data into prioritized next actions: follow-up simulations, department coaching, and policy fixes for the highest-risk workflows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +

Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through the Stripe Customer Portal with a single click. Your premium features will remain active until the end of your billing cycle.

What happens to my simulated employee data? +

We take data security very seriously. All employee lists, campaign click logs, and generated threat briefs are encrypted at rest and in transit. If you choose to delete your account or specific campaigns, all associated data is permanently purged from our database immediately.

Do I need complex technical setup? +

No. You can start simulating immediately using our default simulated delivery mode (Sandbox). To run live campaigns to actual email boxes, all you need is a standard SMTP credential relay, which takes less than 3 minutes to input in your profile preferences.

What is the 50-employee limit on the PRO flat rate plan? +

The flat rate plan covers active simulation tracking for up to 50 individual employees/targets at any given time. If your company size exceeds this limit, contact support to scale to custom volume pricing tailored to your exact headcount.
