Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the name, email address, company domain, and credentials of users signing up to manage campaigns. We also collect target lists uploaded by users containing name, email, department, and title.

2. Telemetry Logging

For simulated phishing campaigns, we log open events, click events, and report events. We do **not** capture or store raw credentials or sensitive passwords submitted during simulations.

3. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. Campaign data is segregated per organization.
