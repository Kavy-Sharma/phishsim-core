1. Data We Collect

We collect the name, email address, company domain, and credentials of administrative users signing up to manage campaigns. We also collect target lists uploaded by customers containing recipient names, emails, department identifiers, and job roles.

All target employee information is processed strictly under customer direction for authorized security awareness assessment and simulation.

2. Activity Logging

For simulated phishing campaigns, we log interaction events: email delivery, open events, link click events, and training reporting events.

Zero Password Capture Policy: We do not store, log, or inspect raw passwords or sensitive credentials submitted during simulations. When a user submits credentials on a simulated fake login portal, the input is immediately discarded in volatile memory and only an anonymized boolean indicator (e.g. credentials_entered = True) is recorded for scoring purposes.

3. Target Data Isolation

Every organization operates within an isolated tenant boundary. Campaign configurations, employee rosters, and simulation event logs are partitioned by organization ID and cannot be accessed across tenant boundaries.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Database instances and cloud infrastructure are hosted in SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified data center facilities with continuous vulnerability scanning and strict access controls.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Customers maintain full ownership of their data. Simulation campaign logs are retained for the duration of the active subscription or according to customer-configured retention windows (e.g., 90 days, 180 days, or 1 year). Upon account termination or written request, all associated target data and event logs are permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights & Contacts

Data subjects have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of personal information processed by PhishSim AI. For all privacy inquiries, please contact our data protection team at privacy@phishsim.ai.
