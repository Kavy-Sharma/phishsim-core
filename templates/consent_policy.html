Authorized Use Only

Last updated: June 2026

1. Explicit Authorization Required

PhishSim.ai is a security testing utility. You must obtain clear, written consent from the executive leadership or designated security authority of the target organization prior to uploading targets or initiating campaigns.

2. Scope of Consent

Consent must cover active domain verification, simulated message dispatching, and tracking metrics. Tests targeting external vendors, partners, or third-party entities are strictly outside the scope of authorized usage.

3. Legal Compliance

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their security simulations comply with local, national, and international cybersecurity testing guidelines. PhishSim.ai accepts no liability for tests conducted without valid organizational authorization.
